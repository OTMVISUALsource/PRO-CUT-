How PRO CUT works

1

Drop your video

Drag an MP4 or MOV onto the page, or tap to pick one. PRO CUT scans it right away.

2

Watch the preview

Press play. The red and purple parts are skipped automatically, so you hear the finished edit. Tap any block on the timeline to bring it back.

3

Export

Open the Export tab and hit Export video. When it finishes, tap Save video.

What it does

  • Pauses: measures loudness 100 times a second, learns your room’s quiet level, and cuts any silence longer than your setting.
  • Umm / uhh: looks for short, steady, flat-sounding blips between pauses. These are marked purple. It listens to sound, not words, so check them in preview.
  • Smooth cuts: keeps a little breath room at each edge and fades audio for a few milliseconds so there are no clicks.
  • Voice: removes steady background noise, cuts muddiness, adds presence and air, then levels everything to a consistent volume.

Tips

  • Cutting a word? Lower Silence level or raise Breath room.
  • Pauses missed in a noisy room? Raise Silence level.
  • Noise reduction works best on hiss, fans and hum. It can’t fully remove music or other voices.
  • Shortcuts: Space play, S skip cuts, A before/after, , . jump between cuts.
  • Works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari 17+. Videos under 30 minutes are smoothest on phones.
