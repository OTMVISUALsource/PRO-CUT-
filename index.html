How PRO CUT works

1

Drop your video

Drag in an MP4 or MOV, or tap to pick one from your gallery. iPhone HEVC videos work too. PRO CUT scans it right away.

2

Preview the edit

Press play. Red and purple parts are skipped, so you hear the finished cut. Tap any block on the timeline to bring it back.

3

Enhance (optional)

Open Enhance, pick a look and an upscale size. Drag the line on the video to compare before and after.

4

Export

In Export, choose a quality and tap Export video. On a phone, tap Save to Photos when it’s done.

What it does

  • Pauses: measures loudness 100 times a second, learns your room’s quiet level, and cuts any silence longer than your setting.
  • Umm / uhh: looks for short, steady, flat-sounding blips between pauses. It listens to sound, not words, so check the purple marks.
  • Voice: removes steady background noise, cuts muddiness, adds presence and air, then levels the volume.
  • Enhance: sharpens edges, smooths grain, lifts color and can upscale to 1080p, 1440p or 4K.
  • Export: decodes every original frame and re-encodes it as H.264 MP4 at a high bitrate, so the cut is frame-accurate and holds up after uploading.

Quality guide

  • High: great for quick posts, smaller files.
  • Ultra: best for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
  • Master: near-lossless for editing again in CapCut, Final Cut or DaVinci. Big files.

Tips

  • Cutting a word? Lower Silence level or raise Breath room.
  • Pauses missed in a noisy room? Raise Silence level.
  • Noise reduction works best on hiss, fans and hum. It can’t fully remove music or other voices.
  • Shortcuts: Space play, S skip cuts, A before/after, , . jump between cuts.
  • Pro camera files (Blackmagic Camera, 4K 10-bit 4:2:2 HEVC) open fastest in Safari on iPhone or Mac. Other browsers build a preview on your device first, which takes a few minutes.
  • Picture black or stuttering? Tap Build a compatible preview under the video.
  • Best in Safari 17+, Chrome or Edge. Keep the tab open while exporting.
